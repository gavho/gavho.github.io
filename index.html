Selected work

Projects & research

Research · Lead Dec 2024

Digital Geospatial Analysis of Operation Barbarossa (1941)

Historical GIS reconstruction of the 1941 invasion of the Soviet Union. I led a team of undergraduate researchers through digitization and built Python workflows that processed 370+ feature classes and about 10,000 records, using divisional clustering, path generation and center-of-mass analysis. Presented at The Dupuy Institute's Historical Analysis Annual Conference.

  • Python
  • ArcGIS Pro
  • Spatial clustering
  • Team lead
Hydrology Sep — Dec 2024

Airport Flood Risk Assessment: Jasper County Airport

Flood risk and watershed analysis in ArcGIS Pro 3.3. I simulated several rainfall intensity scenarios, delineated watersheds from high-resolution DEMs and UAS data, and presented the flood hazard maps in an ArcGIS StoryMap. Follow-up article: "Rainfall and Runways: A Deep Dive into Flood Risks at Rural Airports."

  • ArcGIS Pro
  • DEM
  • StoryMap
UAS Survey Oct — Nov 2024

Jasper County Airport Digital Twin

Assistant Project Lead on a 30-person team that built a 3D digital twin of the airport. I handled mission planning, regulatory compliance and data management, and coordinated the RGB and LiDAR aerial surveys.

  • LiDAR
  • Photogrammetry
  • Part 107
Capstone · Hardware Aug 2024 — May 2025

Drone Delivery for Great Lakes Maritime Logistics

A drone package delivery system for ships on the Great Lakes. I did the mechanical and electrical design of a gravity-based drop system with a servo-actuated release, modeled it in Siemens NX and built an Arduino-based controller. Proof-of-concept deliveries flew over 1.8 miles.

  • Siemens NX
  • Arduino
  • UAS design
  • Flight test
