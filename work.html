01 · Purdue FORCES Initiative

Reconstructing Operation Barbarossa with spatial data science

Purdue's FORCES Initiative (Strategy, Security & Social Systems) studies how leaders make decisions under uncertainty. I led the geospatial side of its Operation Barbarossa project, turning WWII-era operational maps into an analyzable, queryable dataset of the 1941 invasion of the Soviet Union.

ArcGIS Experience · Interactive dashboard Operation Barbarossa Divisional movements for Army Groups North, Center and South
Open full screen ↗
Graduate researcher · GIS project manager 2024 — 2025

The source material was a stack of wartime operational maps: hand-drawn, inconsistently projected and never meant to be analyzed by a computer. I wrote the protocol for georeferencing and digitizing them, managed a remote team of six analysts through production, and built Python workflows that turned their output into a clean spatiotemporal dataset. The result supports questions the paper maps couldn't answer, such as how fast each corridor advanced and where formations bunched up.

  4. 04Analytics: divisional clustering, path and average-path generation, and center-of-mass tracking
  5. 05Published as an interactive ArcGIS Experience and presented at The Dupuy Institute's Historical Analysis Annual Conference

03 · Systems engineering

Drone delivery to moving vessels

Senior capstone, Aug 2024 — May 2025. The Great Lakes shipping fleet has no fast way to get small parts and supplies to a ship underway. Our team designed and flight-tested a drone payload release system to deliver them.

Flight test footage Watch the payload drop tests View on LinkedIn ↗
Mechanical & electrical design lead Purdue UAS capstone

I led the mechanical and electrical design of a gravity-based drop mechanism. A servo-actuated pin holds the payload until a long-range wireless command releases it. I modeled the housing in Siemens NX as a modular mount for the DJI Matrice 300, tuned to keep the aircraft's center of gravity stable and isolate the electronics from vibration.

  1. 01Requirements set by the use case: delivering to a vessel that is underway

  3. 03Arduino control system with long-range wireless triggering
  4. 04Bench testing, then iterative flight tests to refine the release
  5. 05Proof-of-concept deliveries completed at distances over 1.8 miles
